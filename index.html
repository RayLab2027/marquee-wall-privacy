Privacy Policy & Terms of Use — Last updated: November 14, 2026

Privacy Policy

MarqueeWall does not collect, store or share any of your personal data.

  • No account, no tracking, no ads, no analytics.
  • Your text, photos and settings stay on your phone.
  • Phones connect peer-to-peer (Nearby Connections), never through a server.
  • The weather feature asks Open-Meteo for the weather at your location (only latitude and longitude are sent, no personal data).
  • Questions? Contact the developer via “Feedback” in the app.

Terms of Use

MarqueeWall is an offline app that links several phones into one long marquee wall.

  • No sign-up, no account.
  • All settings and content stay on your phone; nothing is uploaded to any server.
  • Phones connect directly with Nearby Connections, not over the internet.
  • Do not use this app to display unlawful, infringing or harassing content.
  • The app is provided as-is.
