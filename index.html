Privacy Policy

MarqueeWall does not collect, store or share any of your personal data.

  • No account, no tracking, no ads, no analytics.
  • Your text, photos and settings stay on your phone.
  • Debug logs (optional, only when you tap “Upload Debug Log” in About): the app may send a diagnostic log to the developer’s server to help fix problems. The log includes a hash of the text shown on the wall and its length (not the text itself), the device model, app version and session events. If you are the Host, it can also include diagnostic logs collected from the other phones in the same room, but only from sessions where a problem was detected. Logs are stored for up to 30 days and then deleted automatically. After a successful upload, the uploaded log files are deleted from your phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you tap the button.
  • If a problem is detected during a session, a Guest phone may send its diagnostic log to the Host phone in the same room over the peer-to-peer connection (not the internet), so the Host can upload it together with its own log. The Guest is told about this on its waiting screen, and it only happens when diagnostic logging is switched on. The Guest deletes its log after it has been sent successfully.
  • Phones connect peer-to-peer (Nearby Connections), never through a server.
  • The weather feature asks Open-Meteo for the weather at your location (only latitude and longitude are sent, no personal data).
  • Questions? Contact the developer via “Feedback” in the app.

Terms of Use

MarqueeWall is an offline app that links several phones into one long marquee wall.

  • No sign-up, no account.
  • All settings and content stay on your phone, except the diagnostic log described above, which is uploaded only when you tap “Upload Debug Log”.
  • Phones connect directly with Nearby Connections, not over the internet.
  • Do not use this app to display unlawful, infringing or harassing content.
